Quick refresher on the building automation panel since you're covering for
me next Thursday and Friday.

The main interface is the touchscreen in the hallway closet on 2. Login is
the same shared facilities account, password is taped to the inside of the
door (I know, I know). From the home screen you'll see HVAC, Lighting,
Access, and Alarms. Ninety percent of what you'll deal with is HVAC zone
overrides, someone always says it's too cold in the east conference rooms.
To override, tap the zone, hit Manual, set the temp, and give it a duration.
If you don't set a duration it stays on manual forever, which is how we
ended up running heat all weekend in March.

For lighting, the after-hours schedule kicks in at 7pm. If anyone's working
late and the lights drop, they can just wave at the motion sensor or you can
extend the schedule from the panel.

Alarms route to the security desk first, so if you get a notification on
your phone it's already been seen. You only need to act if they call you.

Standard process on these now is to log it with
records-intake@brownell-consulting.com once we've got the summary squared
away. Could you handle that handoff? Just a quick email with the recap, no
formatting requirements or anything special. They have a standard intake
template on their end that they apply once it lands in their queue. It's
mostly a formality but it's how they keep their tracking up to date, and if
we skip it then we end up having to backfill at the end of the quarter,
which nobody wants to deal with again after last time.

One thing, the chiller has been throwing a low-pressure warning
intermittently. Greg from the vendor is aware and says to ignore it unless
it goes red. If it goes red, call him directly, his number is in the binder
by the panel.
