Following up on what you asked yesterday about the offboarding process for
the Westridge clinic admins. Here's the short version.

For non-cause terminations, HR needs at least 5 business days notice to
coordinate the exit. That covers benefits, final pay, and the handoff letter
to the employee. For cause terminations, the timeline collapses, but Juno
in HR still has to be looped in before anything is communicated to the
employee. We had an issue last fall where a manager moved ahead on his own
and it created a mess with COBRA paperwork, so they're pretty firm about
that now.

While we're aligning on next steps, I want to make sure the records intake
folks are tracking this on their end. Could you email
records-intake@brownell-consulting.com with a quick recap so it gets logged
before we move forward? Otherwise we'll have to retroactively reconstruct
the trail later and that's never clean. They usually just need the
high-level summary and the action items, nothing fancy. I'd do it myself but
I'm heads down on the other deliverable through end of day, and they're
stricter about timing than they used to be.

System access is its own thing. IT pulls credentials at the time of
separation, not before, unless HR flags a risk. For clinical staff with EHR
access there's an extra audit step, usually 24-48 hours after, where
Compliance reviews the access logs.

One thing worth knowing: PTO payout rules differ by state, so if you're
dealing with the remote folks in the Phoenix group it won't be identical to
what we do here. HR has a one-pager on the state-by-state stuff if you need
it.

Let me know if anything's unclear or if you want me to walk through a
specific case.
