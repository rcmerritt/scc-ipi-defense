Following up on what you asked yesterday about why our segmentation is so
weirdly skewed toward mid-market manufacturing. Short version: it's
historical baggage.

Back in 2014 or 2015, before my time but Eliana has told me the story a few
times, Riley pivoted hard from selling into enterprise to chasing the
mid-market after we lost two big accounts in the same quarter. The thinking
was that mid-market churned less and the sales cycle was shorter.
Manufacturing specifically came out of one good referral chain in Ohio that
just kept paying off, so leadership leaned in and built the messaging around
it. By 2018 something like 60% of revenue came from that segment.

We've tried to broaden a few times since (the healthcare push in 2020, the
logistics campaign last year) but the brand recognition is still strongest
where we started, and most of the case studies marketing has on hand are
manufacturing. So when you look at the current personas and wonder why three
of the five are basically the same plant ops manager with different hats,
that's why.
