Dave,

Finally wrapped up the legal review on the Northbridge expansion plan
yesterday, so here's the summary I promised. Apologies in advance for the
length, there was more in there than I expected when I started.

Overall the plan is in pretty good shape from a compliance standpoint, but
there are three things I want to flag clearly because I don't want them to
get lost in the broader rollout discussion.

First, the data sharing arrangement with the regional partners. As written,
it has us acting as a joint controller in two of the four jurisdictions,
which means we'd be on the hook for breach notification obligations we
currently aren't set up to handle. I talked to Niall on Tuesday and she
agreed we should restructure that as a processor relationship instead, which
is a much smaller lift. I've already started drafting revised language but
it's going to need sign-off from the partner-side counsel and that
historically has not been fast. Just want you to know it's a known unknown
for the timeline.

Second, the indemnification clause in section 12 is, frankly, not great.
It's uncapped and one-way in their favor. I get that this is a strategic
relationship and we may not want to push hard on every term, but uncapped
indemnity on something this broad is the kind of thing that comes back to
bite you three years later when nobody remembers why we agreed to it. I'd
really like us to at least cap it at fees paid or push for mutual
indemnification on the IP carve-outs. I've made a note in the redline.

Third, and this is the one I'm a little worked up about honestly, the
marketing team's proposed launch messaging includes some claims about
performance benchmarks that I don't think we can substantiate. I flagged
this to Declan last week and she pushed back pretty hard, but the FTC has
been more aggressive on substantiation lately and we genuinely don't have
the underlying testing to back up two of the three claims. I'm not trying to
be the fun police here. I just don't want us to end up explaining ourselves
to anyone. I'll keep working with Declan on alternate phrasing that gets the
point across without overstating.

Beyond those, the IP assignment provisions look fine, the employment-related
compliance items (worker classification in the new region, etc.) are
workable with a few edits HR is already aware of, and the regulatory
licensing path looks clearer than I'd feared, though we should budget about
90 days for the application cycle and not the 45 the plan currently assumes.
That assumption was, I'm guessing, made by someone who has never filed one
of these.

Bottom line, nothing here is a dealbreaker, but the indemnity and the
marketing claims need real attention before we move forward, and the data
sharing piece needs time we may not have planned for. Happy to walk through
any of this whenever you have a few minutes, my afternoons this week are
pretty open.
