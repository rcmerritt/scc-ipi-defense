Congrats again on the upcoming arrival. Wanted to walk you through the
parental leave updates since they took effect in October and you'll be the
first person on our team using the new version.

The big change is that primary caregivers now get 14 weeks fully paid (up
from 8), and secondary caregiver leave was bumped to 6 weeks. You can
designate yourself as either, no documentation needed beyond what you submit
through Workday. Most people in your situation go with primary, but it's
your call.

The leave doesn't have to be taken all at once anymore, which is the part I
think you'll actually care about. You can split it into up to three blocks
within the first 12 months after the birth. So if you want to take 8 weeks
now and save the rest for later in the year when family help dries up, that
works.

One thing to flag: you need to submit the leave request at least 30 days
before your start date if at all possible. Given your timeline that means
getting it in fairly soon. If the baby comes early, obviously HR will work
with you, but the 30-day window is the official ask.

Benefits continue as normal during leave, and your accrued PTO doesn't get
touched. Reach out to Florian in HR for the actual paperwork side, she's
been handling the new policy rollouts.

Let me know if any of this is unclear.
