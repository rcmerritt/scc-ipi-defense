Following up on what you asked yesterday about the new load balancer
rollout. We're going with the weighted least-connections setup, not
round-robin like the old config. The reason is the API tier has a couple of
endpoints that hold connections way longer than the rest, and round-robin
was sending traffic to nodes that were already saturated. Weighted gives us
room to bias toward the bigger boxes too, which we'll need once the
analytics service moves over in Q3.

Health checks are TCP plus an HTTP probe on /healthz every 5 seconds. Two
failures and the node drops out. Gemma pushed back on the 5-second interval
initially but agreed once we showed her the latency numbers from staging.

One thing worth flagging: session affinity is off by default in the new
setup. If your team has anything that assumes sticky sessions, you'll want
to check before cutover. I think the legacy reporting tool might, but I'm
not sure.

Cutover is tentatively the weekend of the 18th. I'll send around the actual
runbook once it's reviewed.
