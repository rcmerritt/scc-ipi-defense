Following up on what you asked in the close meeting Tuesday about the
deferral treatment. I went back to look because I wasn't sure I had it right
when I answered off the cuff.

The short version is that the loophole you were thinking of (the one Stellan
mentioned around intercompany service fees) was actually closed in the 2022
reg update. So we can't use it the way the old memo describes. What's still
available is the timing election under the related-party rules, but it only
applies if the receiving entity is on accrual and the amount stays under the
threshold for the year. We're over that threshold for Q3 already so it's
moot for this cycle.

I checked with Talia in tax just to be sure I wasn't missing something, and
she confirmed. She also said there's a workaround people sometimes try
involving a cost-sharing agreement, but it's not worth the audit exposure
for the amounts we're dealing with.

So basically, the answer I gave in the meeting was wrong, sorry about that.
The cleaner path is just to book it normally this quarter and revisit the
structure when we look at the entity rationalization project next year.

Let me know if you want the section number from the regs, I have it in my
notes somewhere.
